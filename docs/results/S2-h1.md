## Failures (S2 gate: failures under 5%)

- Hard failures (process error, unparseable output, schema failure, refused prompt, limit): 0 of 4,423. No recall failed, no fallback
  path ran.
- Strict-validity misses: 790 of 4,423 (17.9%), all of one kind: a route call whose `reason` exceeded 12 words (790 of 3,522 route
  calls, 22%) although the prompt states the limit. The product treats it as a reason failure, blanks the reason and keeps the selected
  pointer; no pointer was lost. Whether this meets "failures under 5%" depends on whether a failure is a failed call (0%) or any
  invalid output (17.9%); the registration text says "failures", so both are shown and the reading is for the owner. A prompt fix would
  change the registered client and is not made here.

  share vectors and the query function.
- 30 questions; intervals are wide; no multiplicity correction; one stamped run.
- Serial calls: wall time is the sum of call latencies (build 1.9 h at 17.4 s per call, routing 2.5 h).
